# PowerBI-project-HR-Analytics
This Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of employee distribution, attrition patterns, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, overtime, promotions, and business travel across departments. The report helps HR teams and management identify key factors affecting employee retention and workforce performance, enabling data-driven HR decisions. The purpose of this project is to analyze employee behavior, attrition trends, and workforce performance using interactive visual dashboards. It helps organizations monitor HR metrics, improve employee retention strategies, and support strategic workforce planning through data-driven insights.

Key Insights from the Report
Research & Development department has the highest number of employees and attrition cases.
Employees in roles such as Laboratory Technician and Sales Executive show higher attrition rates.
Most employees belong to the Life Sciences and Medical education fields.
A significant portion of employees work overtime, which may influence attrition.
Majority of employees live within 10 km of the office.
Employees with fewer promotions over several years indicate possible career growth concerns.
Workforce age distribution is highest in the 30–40 age group.
Business travel frequency and job satisfaction levels are analyzed to understand employee engagement trends.

Technologies Used
Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development and data visualization
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Calculated measures and KPIs
Excel / CSV Dataset – Data source for HR records
